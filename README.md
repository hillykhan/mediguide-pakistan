MediGuide Pakistan – AI-Powered Medicine Information Assistant

Final project for the Building AI course

Summary

MediGuide Pakistan is an AI-powered assistant that helps people understand medicine information in simple English and Urdu. It aims to make reliable medicine information more accessible while encouraging users to consult qualified healthcare professionals.

Background

Many people in Pakistan struggle to understand medicine labels, medical terminology, possible side effects, and precautions. This can be especially challenging for people in areas where access to reliable health information is limited.

The project aims to address three problems:

- Difficulty understanding medicine information and medical terminology.
- Limited access to reliable medicine information in local languages.
- Risk of relying on unverified advice from social media or other unreliable sources.

My motivation comes from my interest in Artificial Intelligence and healthcare technology. I believe AI can help make trustworthy information easier to access, particularly for communities with limited healthcare resources.

The goal is not to replace doctors or pharmacists. It is to help people understand information from reliable sources.

How is it used?

A user opens the MediGuide Pakistan application and searches for a medicine by its name.

The system provides:

1. A simple explanation of the medicine's general purpose.
2. Available information about warnings and precautions.
3. Commonly documented side effects.
4. Important information about interactions, where reliable data is available.
5. An easy-to-understand explanation in English or Urdu.
6. Links or references to the original information sources.

The intended users include patients, students, caregivers, and people who want to understand medicine information.

If a question requires medical judgment, the application will advise the user to contact a qualified doctor or pharmacist.

Data sources and AI methods

The project could use publicly available and appropriately licensed medicine information from sources such as:

- "Drug Regulatory Authority of Pakistan (DRAP)" (https://www.dra.gov.pk/)
- "World Health Organization (WHO)" (https://www.who.int/)
- "MedlinePlus" (https://medlineplus.gov/)
- Official medicine leaflets and product information, subject to permission and licensing.

AI techniques

The proposed system could use:

- Natural Language Processing (NLP): To understand users' questions.
- Text classification: To categorize medicine-related questions.
- TF-IDF and similarity methods: To find relevant information from a collection of medicine documents.
- Nearest-neighbor search: To identify documents that are similar to a user's query.
- Information retrieval: To retrieve relevant information from trusted sources.
- Machine translation: To help explain information in Urdu and English, with human review for accuracy.

The initial prototype could use Python and a small, carefully verified collection of medicine information.

Challenges

MediGuide Pakistan has important limitations:

- AI-generated information may be incomplete or incorrect.
- Medicine information must be updated regularly.
- Urdu translations of medical terminology may not always be accurate.
- Reliable and legally reusable datasets may be difficult to obtain.
- Different patients may have different medical conditions, allergies, and medication requirements.
- The system cannot diagnose diseases, prescribe medicines, or determine individual dosages.

Privacy, data protection, transparency, and medical safety must be considered throughout development.

The system must clearly explain that it provides educational information and does not replace professional medical advice.

What next?

Possible future improvements include:

1. Developing a working web or mobile application.
2. Expanding the medicine information database using verified sources.
3. Improving Urdu-language understanding and translation.
4. Adding a document-based question-answering system with source citations.
5. Testing the system with healthcare professionals.
6. Evaluating the accuracy, reliability, and usability of its answers.
7. Collaborating with pharmacists, doctors, and AI researchers in Pakistan.

My long-term goal is to develop a trustworthy and accessible healthcare information platform that can support people across Pakistan.
